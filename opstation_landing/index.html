— One platform

Every part of the business, in one connected system.

Each module shares the same products, branches, customers and ledger — so a sale, a receipt or a production batch updates everything at once.

Inventory & stock

Multi-branch stock, transfers, opening balances, valuation and adjustments — with a live inventory ledger.

Purchasing

The full PO → GRN → Purchase Invoice flow with suppliers, bulk import and a pending-stage dashboard.

Sales

Sales Order → Delivery Order → Sales Invoice, returns, promoter commissions and a sales report with drill-down.

Point of sale

A branch-isolated POS with its own catalog, held bills, expenses and customer history — posting straight to the GL.

Manufacturing

Bills of material, job cards, QC checkpoints and a live production floor with per-batch costing.

Financials

Accrual general ledger, vouchers, customer & supplier ledgers, aging, trial balance, P&L and balance sheet.

Assets & facility

An asset register with QR datasheets, plus scheduled facility maintenance, areas and reminders.

Field operations built in

— Control & oversight

Tight control over every branch, down to the voucher.

Hand each person exactly the access they need, watch every change as it happens, and keep a tight grip on the books — across every branch.

Granular role-based access

Grant permissions module by module, and scope what each user sees down to the branch.

Full audit trail

Every create, edit and void is logged with who did it and when — nothing changes off the record.

Approvals & locking

Finalize a document to lock it; after that, only authorized roles can reopen or reverse it.

Live pending dashboards

— Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is Opstation built for more than one branch?+

Yes. Branches are first-class — stock, POS, deliveries and reporting are all branch-aware, while the ledger consolidates at the organization level.

Does it handle accounting, or just operations?+

Both. Every document — sales, purchases, POS, production, adjustments — posts to a real accrual general ledger, so financial statements stay live.

Can I control who sees what?+

Access is role-based and scoped by branch, and a full audit trail records who changed what, and when.

How do I get started?+

Tap Sign up, tell us about your business, and we'll set up your organization. Existing users sign in at the top.
