— Good to know

Questions, answered.

Does it work with more than one branch?+

Yes. Stock, POS, deliveries and reports all work branch by branch, while the books come together for the whole business.

Does it handle accounting, or just operations?+

Both. Every purchase, sale, delivery, receipt and adjustment posts to a full general ledger, so your statements are always current.

Does the app work without internet?+

Yes. Visits, orders and collections are saved on the phone and sync on their own when the connection is back.

Can I control who sees what?+

Access is set by role and branch, and a full audit trail records who changed what, and when.
