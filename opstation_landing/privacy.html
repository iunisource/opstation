Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Applies to the Opstation web panel and the Opstation mobile app

Opstation is a business system used by trading and distribution companies (“organizations”) to run purchasing, inventory, sales, field visits, deliveries and accounting. This policy explains what information Opstation collects, why, and how it is protected.

In short: Opstation is used by organizations for their own work. Each organization’s information is kept separate and is visible only to that organization’s authorized users. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Who is responsible

The organization that gives you an Opstation account (your employer or business) decides what information is entered and who can see it. Opstation provides and runs the service on that organization’s behalf. Questions about your organization’s records are best directed to its administrator; questions about this policy can be sent to us (see “Contact”).

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email or login code, phone number, role, and the organization and branch you belong to.
  • Business records your organization enters: customers and suppliers, products, prices, orders, invoices, payments, receipts, stock and accounting entries.
  • Location (mobile app): the phone’s location is read at specific moments of work — signing in, starting and ending a route or delivery, marking a visit, entering a receipt, and saving a shop’s location — and while the route guide or map is open. It is used to confirm visits at the right place, measure route distance for reimbursement, and show the team’s progress to the organization’s managers. Location is not collected when you are not using these features.
  • Photos (mobile app): pictures you choose to take in the app, such as a shop front, receipt or delivery proof.
  • Device information: phone model, Android version, app version and an app-generated device identifier, used to keep one approved phone per login, show which app version is in use and help with support.
  • Notifications: a push-notification token so the app can deliver work alerts.
  • Crash and error reports: technical details when the app or website fails (for example the screen and error message), used only to find and fix problems.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the service: record and show orders, visits, deliveries, receipts, reports and accounts for your organization.
  • To verify field work: check that a visit or receipt was entered at the shop, and work out distances and time in market.
  • To send messages your organization has turned on, such as a receipt confirmation SMS to a customer through the organization’s SMS provider, and work notifications to staff.
  • To keep the service secure and reliable: sign-in protection, device approval, backups and troubleshooting.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only:

  • Within your organization — with users your organization has authorized, according to the permissions it sets.
  • With service providers that run parts of the service for us, under their own security and confidentiality terms: cloud database and hosting (Supabase, Google Firebase), maps and road distances (Google Maps Platform), push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), error reporting (Sentry), and the SMS provider an organization chooses for its customer messages.
  • When required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of users and the service.

5. Data security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each organization’s data is separated in the database and access is limited by role and permission. Backups are taken regularly. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorized access.

6. Retention and deletion

Information is kept for as long as the organization uses Opstation, because business and accounting records must be kept. To delete your user account or personal information, ask your organization’s administrator or contact us. When an organization closes its account, its data is deleted after any period the organization agrees with us or the law requires.

7. Your choices

  • You can allow or deny location, camera and notification permissions in your phone settings. Some features — such as marking visits and entering receipts — need location to work.
  • You can ask your organization’s administrator, or us, to see, correct or delete your personal information.

8. Children

Opstation is a business tool for organizations and their staff. It is not meant for children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated; important changes will be announced in the app or on this site.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please reach us through the Talk to us form on this website, or ask your organization’s administrator to contact us.
